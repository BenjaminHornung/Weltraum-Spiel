# Spielkonzept: Worldbuilding, Factions And Economy

Stand: 2026-06-14
Status: Konzeptbasis fuer Systemidentitaet, Factions, Konflikte und Economy Hooks
Bezug: Baut auf dem Aurelia-Startsystem, Hestia-Konzept, Ressourcen, Drones, Ship Builder und spaeterem Planetary Gameplay auf.

## 1. Ziel

Worldbuilding soll erklaeren, warum der Spieler im Aurelia-System fliegt, landet, minet, handelt, kaempft und Schiffe baut. Factions und Economy sind keine getrennte Lore-Schicht, sondern Gruende fuer Orte, Preise, Konflikte, Missionen, Ausruestung und Risiken.

Leitsatz:

> The world should make the space loop feel necessary: resources create power, power creates conflict, conflict creates work for independent pilots.

## 2. Warum dieses System als Gameplay Space existiert

Das Aurelia-System ist wertvoll, aber nicht vollstaendig kontrolliert:

- Hestia ist ein einzigartiger Lebensplanet mit hoher Forschung, Bioressourcen und Schutzkonflikten.
- Tharos und die Monde bieten Kolonie-, Eis- und Outpost-Potential.
- Der Asteroidenguertel liefert Metalle, Eis, Kohlenstoff und Stationen.
- Gas- und Eisriesen bieten spaetere Energie-, Isotop- und Slingshot-Rollen.
- Alte automatisierte Infrastruktur oder verlassene Anlagen deuten auf fruehere Expeditionen hin.
- Grosse Entfernungen machen unabhaengige Piloten, Drohnen und autonome Logistik wertvoll.

Das System ist kein voll erschlossener Kernsektor. Es ist eine Grenze: genug Ordnung fuer Handel, genug Luecken fuer Risiko.

## 3. Warum Ressourcen matter

Ressourcen sind Macht:

- Fuel bestimmt Reichweite.
- Metalle bestimmen Schiffe, Stationen und Waffen.
- Electronics bestimmen Autopilot, Drones, Scanner und Ship Builder.
- Exotics bestimmen High-End Upgrades.
- Biological Samples bestimmen Forschung, Medizin und Kontroversen.
- Ammo materials bestimmen Kampffaehigkeit.
- Construction materials bestimmen Outposts und Infrastruktur.

Wer Ressourcen kontrolliert, kontrolliert Routen, Preise, Jobs, Sicherheit und politische Ansprueche.

## 4. Grundkonflikte

| Conflict | Gameplay effect |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate extraction vs frontier survival | Claims, permits, sabotage, labor disputes, smuggling. |
| Science protection vs profit mining | Hestia preserves, illegal samples, research contracts. |
| Security authority vs independent pilots | Inspections, bounties, licenses, contraband. |
| Settlers vs pirates | Defense missions, rescue, convoy escort, salvage. |
| Salvage law vs survival pragmatism | Wreck ownership, black market, reputation tradeoffs. |
| Old automated systems vs current factions | Ruins, defense drones, mystery sites, high-risk rewards. |

## 7. Independent Pilots, Builders And Miners

Independent players exist because the system has too many small jobs and too much distance for big factions to solve efficiently:

- deliver parts to remote outposts,
- survey new claims,
- salvage contested wrecks,
- escort drones,
- mine small rich nodes,
- repair stranded ships,
- transport illegal or sensitive cargo,
- test experimental ship modules,
- scout landing sites,
- respond faster than corporate logistics.

The player is not the chosen one. The player is useful because a ship, a suit, drones, tools and risk tolerance can turn unknown places into value.

## 8. Economy Hooks

### Resource Supply And Demand

Prices should vary by location:

- water is cheap on ice worlds, expensive in hot inner zones,
- refined metals are cheap near corporate refineries, expensive at frontier outposts,
- ammo spikes in conflict zones,
- biological samples are valuable near science labs,
- fuel is expensive far from depots,
- salvage parts gain value after battles or disasters.

### Ship Parts Market

Ship Builder progression needs:

- structural frames,
- thrusters,
- RCS parts,
- weapon mounts,
- sensors,
- cargo modules,
- life support,
- landing gear,
- drone bays.

Factions can specialize in part styles and restrictions.

### Fuel And Ammo Market

Fuel and ammo are recurring sinks:

- fuel drives travel range,
- RCS propellant drives precision,
- ammo drives combat risk,
- capacitor cells drive energy weapons,
- explosives are high value and restricted.

### Mining Licenses And Illegal Mining

Mining can be:

- free in unclaimed zones,
- paid license in corporate zones,
- faction reputation gated,
- illegal in protected biomes,
- dangerous in pirate territory,
- contested in abandoned zones.

### Faction Reputation

Reputation affects:

- docking permissions,
- prices,
- missions,
- police response,
- claim access,
- repair/refit priority,
- black-market offers,
- whether a faction warns before firing.

### Salvage Legality

Wrecks need ownership states:

```text
Unclaimed
Abandoned
FactionOwned
PlayerOwned
CriminalEvidence
ProtectedHistorical
BlackMarketOnly
```

### Black Market

Black markets buy:

- stolen electronics,
- illegal biological samples,
- unlicensed exotics,
- restricted weapons,
- pirate transponders,
- old AI cores.

They also sell risk: better margins, worse reputation consequences.

### Repair And Refit Costs

Every expedition should be weighed against:

- fuel spent,
- ammo spent,
- tool wear,
- drone repairs,
- hull repairs,
- suit filters,
- cargo opportunity cost.

## 9. Environmental Storytelling

Worldbuilding should often be delivered without dialogue:

- damaged mining rigs showing a failed extraction,
- faction color codes on crates and doors,
- log snippets near abandoned bunks,
- cargo manifests,
- emergency beacons,
- patched hulls and improvised settlement walls,
- legal warning signs,
- dead drones around old defense systems,
- biological overgrowth on Hestia ruins,
- scorch marks from ship weapons where they were overused.

The player should infer who was here, what they wanted, and what went wrong.

## 10. V0 Without Full RPG Complexity

V0 worldbuilding can be simple:

- faction owner label on sites,
- basic trade terminal,
- simple mission board,
- one reputation number or relation state,
- cargo buy/sell prices,
- legal/illegal warning on scanner,
- one outpost with storage and refuel,
- one hostile pirate or security site.

NPC conversation trees are future scope. The first version can use terminals, radio messages and clear location design.

## 11. Integration With Ships, Drones, Autopilot And Builder

- Ships carry cargo, fuel, weapons, drones and landing capability.
- Drones extend survey, mining and cargo reach.
- Autopilot gets the player to orbit targets, landing zones, outposts and pickup points.
- Ship Builder consumes resources and faction parts.
- Economy creates reasons to haul, refit and choose routes.
- Factions create permission, danger and mission context.
- Planet sites generate discoveries that become map targets and route plans.

## 12. Kurzfazit

The Aurelia-System works best as a frontier with enough order to trade and enough disorder to need independent pilots. Factions turn resources into conflict. Economy turns conflict into choices. Planetary first-person mode becomes the place where those choices are touched directly: a claim marker, a locked crate, a dying outpost, a protected sample, an illegal drill, a cargo hold waiting back at the ship.
