# Capability: Prototype Target Hit Feedback

## Requirement: Targets can receive projectile hits

The prototype SHALL provide simple target dummies that can register projectile hits.

## Requirement: Hits are visible

A hit on a target dummy SHALL produce visible feedback suitable for manual playtesting.

## Requirement: Scope remains prototype-only

The target hit feedback SHALL NOT introduce final combat architecture, multiplayer, inventory, or full damage systems.
