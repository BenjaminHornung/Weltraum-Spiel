# Capability Spec: Browser App Foundation v1


## Requirements

### APP-001
The browser app SHALL be under `apps/weltraum-browser`.

### APP-002
The app SHALL run without Unity.

### APP-003
The app SHALL expose a test bridge for Playwright automation.

### APP-004
The app SHALL retain deterministic fixed-step simulation tests.

### APP-005
The app SHALL produce evidence files for browser scenarios.
