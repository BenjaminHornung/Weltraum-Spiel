# Capability: Flight Tuning Diagnostics

## Requirement: Flight commands are inspectable

The prototype SHALL expose enough diagnostic information to understand active main thrust, RCS, and SAS commands during flight.

## Requirement: Tuning values remain easy to adjust

Important prototype tuning values SHALL remain inspector-adjustable or otherwise clearly visible for iteration.

## Requirement: Debug visuals do not affect physics

Any force or torque visualizations SHALL be debug-only and SHALL NOT change gameplay physics.
