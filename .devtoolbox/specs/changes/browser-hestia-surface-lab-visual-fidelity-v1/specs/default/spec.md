# Capability: Hestia Surface Lab Visual Fidelity

## Requirement: Readable Landform
The accepted default capture shall show a non-empty Hestia region with readable foreground, middle and background elevation bands, a natural hero landmark, visible faceted normals and distinguishable wet-rock, wet-soil, moss and biological presentation.

## Requirement: Atmosphere and Water
Fog shall create depth without obscuring the terrain. Presentation-only water shall read as a restrained shoreline or wetland break, not a hard rectangular slab or authoritative simulation volume.

## Requirement: Vegetation and Palette
Deterministic reconstructable vegetation shall appear in grouped patches with open ground. The scene shall use a dark green, petrol and restrained cyan direction with enough contrast to inspect the generated surface.

## Requirement: Debug Inspection
Wireframe and chunk boundaries shall be visibly distinct when enabled but shall not dominate or erase the terrain silhouette. The quarter-meter capture shall visibly demonstrate coherent finer detail at the approved camera fixture or an explicitly amended shared fixture.

## Requirement: Technical HUD Accessibility
The edge-bound HUD shall keep the center clear, remain usable at 1280x720, expose visible keyboard focus, load its intended fonts without browser decoding errors, and continue to state TECHNICAL PROVING GROUND and NOT GAMEPLAY.

## Requirement: Authority Preservation
Visual work shall not change canonical voxel/mesh hashes, worker/cache behavior, terrain topology, backend contracts or gameplay truth. Concept PNGs shall never be loaded by runtime.

## Requirement: Evidence
Default, wireframe and quarter-meter 1920x1080 captures plus the 1280x720 responsive pass shall be reviewed independently. Visual acceptance requires no open P0-P2 UI findings and shall not be inferred from telemetry alone.
