# Design: Browser Hestia Surface Lab Visual Fidelity V1

## Baseline Finding
The pipeline-baseline screenshots are technically valid but visually blocked: the landform reads as a dark low-contrast slab, water is not a persuasive shoreline, overlay density obscures shape, and the quarter-meter view does not establish readable microvoxel detail. Rajdhani decoding warnings and uncertain visible focus appearance remain open.

## Authority Boundary
Canonical VoxelBrick bytes, density/material generation, Surface Nets topology, representation keys, MeshArtifact content, worker/cache behavior and telemetry remain unchanged. All work is a projection in Surface Lab environment, presentation mapping, CSS/HUD or explicitly approved camera composition.

## Approach
1. Re-evaluate exposure, hemisphere/key/rim balance and fog falloff against the audited screenshots.
2. Improve presentation-only material separation and water footprint without inventing simulated water.
3. Group deterministic reconstructable scatter while preserving open terrain and canonical independence.
4. Tune wireframe/boundaries for depth-aware inspection rather than visual dominance.
5. Repair font loading and keyboard-focus appearance without changing input semantics.
6. Compare all captures at the shared approved camera fixture; any fixture change requires an explicit matrix amendment.

## Risks
- Hiding pipeline defects with visual effects: retain technical wireframe/boundary modes and hash evidence.
- Drifting into concept imitation or fake gameplay: use concepts only for shared direction and keep exclusions visible.
- Changing authority to improve appearance: stop if generator, topology or backend contracts appear necessary.
