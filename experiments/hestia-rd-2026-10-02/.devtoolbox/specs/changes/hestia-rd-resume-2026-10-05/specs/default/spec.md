# Requirements

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: preserve and qualify the existing lab
The restart SHALL preserve original source/evidence and use the frozen product-read SHA. Existing implementations SHALL be reused. REN12 SHALL receive an independently reviewed versioned successor with semantic ROIs and positive/negative native image controls; v1 failure SHALL remain historical failure.

#### Scenario: original bad frame
WHEN vertex colors/AO are removed in a real renderer frame THEN the successor oracle rejects it while unchanged repeat frames remain within tolerance.

### Requirement: shared composed route and usable tools
The lab SHALL implement the mandatory original RD14/15/20/21/23/30/31/32 functions and RD41/42/43 tools through the same modules. RD51 SHALL use one renderer/canvas/loop and verify original cases plus 100 lifecycle transitions. Invalid, stale or unsupported inputs SHALL be explicit.

#### Scenario: rainy shelter opening and attachment replay
WHEN weather transitions to rain and a frozen source opens a roof and detaches an owner THEN shielding/wetness/wind/material/camera projection update consistently and removed decoration does not reappear.

### Requirement: truthful complete planner delivery
Every original core card SHALL have a terminal source-bound outcome with complete case accounting. Comparison recommendations SHALL distinguish ADOPT_CANDIDATE/REJECT/DEFER, unsupported metrics SHALL never become zero, ART SHALL remain PENDING_OWNER and PRODUCT_INTEGRATED SHALL remain false. The final archive SHALL include build/start/stop instructions, working tool URLs, presets, sources, raw data and media sidecars, known limitations, matrix, adoption cards and verified checksums.

#### Scenario: archive reproduction
WHEN the planner archive is inspected and started using its documented commands THEN required build, tools and combined lab work and every card links to source-bound evidence.
