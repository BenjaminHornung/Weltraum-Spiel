3. Plan 3 (Endsession / shell dispose on a dead host) — one confirmed blocker risk in the planned shape, not in the intent. The fix must guard **reads**, not cleanup: `shell.dispose()` has to keep clearing listeners, observers and (on a live host) focus/panel even when `isCurrent()` is false. With that condition, `ownerCurrent=false` / `read throws`, late command completion, live focus cleanup and stale isolation are the right test set; the actual Endsession run remains pending.
